Connect your database.

Connect a Supabase project so your portfolios are saved online and available on your published site.

First, run supabase-setup.sql in your Supabase project’s SQL Editor. Paste the Project URL and public anon/publishable key here to connect this browser. For your published site and public portfolio links, also put those two values in config.js before deploying. Never use a service_role key in the browser.
